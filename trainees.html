CKRT supports predoctoral students, postdoctoral physician-scientists, and postdoctoral PhD scientists pursuing research careers across the KUH disciplines at Yale and UConn Health.

Current trainees

CKRT is a newly funded program and its first cohort has not yet been appointed. At full capacity the program supports four postdoctoral and two predoctoral trainees. Trainee profiles — name, track, mentor, and project focus — will appear here once appointments are confirmed.
